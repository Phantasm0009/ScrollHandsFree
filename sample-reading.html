Sample long page

Open the ScrollHandsFree popup and click Start scrolling. This sample page includes the extension's content script so the popup can control it.

Opening rhythm

Good hands-free reading starts slowly. The goal is not to push text past your eyes, but to give the page a steady pace that feels like a quiet reading assistant.

Use the Reading preset for a normal pace, Slow for dense material, and Fast skim when you are scanning for a section or checking structure.

Reading Focus

Reading Focus dims the page outside a soft horizontal band. It should feel calm, not theatrical. The band is meant to help you keep your place without hiding the page from you.

Try turning Reading Focus on, then adjust speed. If the HUD is enabled, it will show progress and time remaining while staying small enough to avoid covering content.

Voice commands

Voice commands are optional. If you enable them, try saying start, pause, stop, faster, slower, focus on, bigger focus, or bottom.

Auto-scroll still works without microphone permission. The microphone is only used while voice commands are enabled for the current tab.

Smart pauses

Headings and natural breaks give the extension places to breathe. A good pause can make auto-scroll feel much less stubborn and much more like a reading tool.

Use Natural or Deliberate rhythm in options if you want the page to pause around paragraphs, figures, or section changes.

End of page

When you reach the end, ScrollHandsFree can stop, loop to the top, or reverse direction. Stop is the safest reading default; loop and reverse are useful for recipes, notes, and presentations.
