## Short Description

Hands-free reading for long pages with auto-scroll, Reading Focus, optional voice commands, and local privacy-conscious settings.

## Suggested Description

ScrollHandsFree turns long articles, documentation, recipes, study material, and presentation notes into a calm hands-free reading experience.

Core features:
- Start, pause, resume, and stop auto-scrolling.
- Speed presets for Slow, Reading, Fast, and Skim.
- Reading presets for Article, Documentation, Study, Fast skim, Recipe, and Presentation.
- Optional Reading Focus band to dim distractions.
- Optional draggable on-page HUD with pause, stop, speed, direction, progress, and time remaining.
- Optional voice commands for start, pause, stop, faster, slower, direction, top/bottom, and Reading Focus.
- Custom voice aliases, disabled commands, and no-microphone command testing.
- Smart scroll area detection for pages, nested containers, docs panes, recipes, and app layouts.
- Page tools for choosing a scroll area, jumping to the next heading, jumping back one paragraph, and viewing basic accessibility hints.
- Per-site preferences and resume positions stored locally.

Privacy summary:
- No account.
- No analytics or tracking.
- Settings are stored locally.
- Microphone is optional and only used for voice commands.
- Browser speech recognition may be processed by your browser/platform.
