Privacy Policy

ScrollHandsFree is designed to run locally in your browser and avoid collecting personal data.

What is stored

  • Extension settings, such as speed, Reading Focus, HUD, and voice language.
  • Optional per-site preferences, such as blocked sites, selected scroll area, or per-site speed.
  • Optional resume positions for pages you used with ScrollHandsFree.
  • Optional voice command aliases and local usage counters.

This data is stored in Chrome local storage on your device.

What is not collected

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics or tracking are included.
  • No browsing history is sent to a server by this extension.
  • No sale of data.

Voice commands

Voice commands are optional. ScrollHandsFree does not store your voice. Browser speech recognition may be processed by your browser or platform, depending on your browser implementation.

Page access

Page access is used to detect scrollable areas, move the page, show Reading Focus/HUD controls, and display simple accessibility hints. These are basic local checks only, not a WCAG audit. The extension does not need a server for these features.
