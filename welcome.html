ScrollHandsFree

Hands-free reading for long pages, with optional voice commands and local, privacy-conscious settings.

1. Open a long pageArticles, docs, recipes, and study pages work best.
2. Click StartThe extension injects only when you ask it to run.
3. Adjust speedUse presets, the HUD, keyboard shortcuts, or optional voice commands.

No microphone needed for auto-scroll

Voice commands are optional. Auto-scroll, keyboard shortcuts, Reading Focus, and the HUD work without microphone permission.

If you enable voice, ScrollHandsFree does not store your voice. Browser speech recognition may be processed by your browser or platform.

How permissions work

  • Page access is used only to find and move the current scroll area.
  • Settings, per-site preferences, resume positions, aliases, and counters are stored locally.
  • No account, no tracking, no analytics, and no sale of data.
